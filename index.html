Chapter one · Solon, Ohio

It started behind a food counter.

Tuscany, Zoss the Swiss Baker, TJ Wolf chocolate, Sharper Image, Solon Rec — I worked all of them. Then I built Feed Cleveland, a nonprofit that moved surplus food from restaurants and grocers to food banks. Same instinct ever since: find what's being wasted and route it where it matters.

Chapter two · Saturdays in the Shoe

100,000 people. One feeling.

Game on the line, and the whole place comes apart at once. That was my first real lesson in what attention feels like at scale — and I've been chasing that sound professionally ever since.

Chapter three · Hermosa Beach

Then I traded snow for saltwater.

Underground, Sharkeez, Fenner's, the Poop Deck — I learned this town on foot, one block at a time, back when Hermosa was still a little rough around the edges. First real chapter of the LA hustle.

Chapter four · Venice

Silicon Beach runs on attention.

A rooftop over the boardwalk, the skate bowl below, tech money filling in around it. Between the studios and the startups I learned how this city actually buys reach — then co-founded Lazy Boy Agency to put brands inside the picture instead of next to it.
