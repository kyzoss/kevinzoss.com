Chapter one · Solon, Ohio

It started behind a food counter.

I learned how a business actually runs from behind a counter: customers, systems, inventory, waste. Then I built Feed Cleveland, a nonprofit that routed surplus food from restaurants and grocers to food banks. First system I ever built, and it set the pattern for everything after. Find the inefficiency, design the creative fix, accelerate what matters.

Chapter two · Columbus

100,000 people. One feeling.

The roar of the Shoe on gameday was my first lesson in attention at full volume. While I was at Ohio State I was building for the fan cultures underneath it: a Cleveland Cavs site and a music blog that aggregated the emerging scenes. Teaching myself how an audience actually gets built online is what laid the groundwork.

Chapter four · Venice

We built ad tech in a beach house.

Mobile Theory, then Aki. We built and sold mobile ad tech out of Venice beach houses, back when a startup here was a rented house with the boardwalk out front and the skate bowl below. Selling Aki taught me how large corporations ruin elegant, working systems, and it sent me straight back to building.

These days it ships without me.

Partner at Lazy Boy Agency, running media and technology. The buys out front, the systems underneath.
