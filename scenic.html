Chapter one · Ohio

Everything starts with autumn.

Buckeye leaves on a quiet street, big skies over small houses. I grew up in the middle of the country, where you learn early that nothing gets handed to you and everything gets earned.

Chapter two · Saturdays

A hundred thousand people, one feeling.

Saturdays in the Horseshoe were my first lesson in attention: what it sounds like when an idea moves a crowd all at once. I never stopped chasing that sound.

Chapter three · The leap

So I pointed everything west.

One car, a long highway, and the kind of bet you only make when the dream is louder than the doubt: leave what's comfortable, drive toward the work you actually want.

Chapter four · The city of screens

Los Angeles runs on attention.

I learned this city from the inside: the billboards, the buys, the rooms where campaigns get decided. Then I co-founded Lazy Boy Agency and started putting brands inside the picture.

Chapter five · Now

Building worlds of my own.

These days the ideas ship around the clock: campaigns for brands in culture, ventures in the lab, AI teams that build while I sleep. The journey keeps going. Come build a chapter.
