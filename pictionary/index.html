🖼️ Art Show

How to Play

1️⃣

Pass the phone to the artist. Everyone else closes their eyes!

2️⃣

Pick a card. The picture shows what to draw, so little ones don't need to read. Tap 🔈 to hear it quietly.

3️⃣

Put the phone down where everyone can see. Draw with your finger! No letters or numbers allowed. 🤐

4️⃣

Shout your guesses! When someone gets it, the artist taps 🎉 Got it!

⭐

Star Race: the guesser and the artist both get a star. Team Family: everyone works together to fill the rainbow.

💡

Stuck? Hints pop up as the timer runs down. Forgot your word? Hold 👀 Peek.

🎭

Make a Character from silly parts, then stamp it on the page with 🧸 and draw a whole world around it. Turn on Character Adventures and your character shows up in the game: you draw what it's doing!

🎂

Each player's age picks their words and their time. Littles get easy words and extra time.
