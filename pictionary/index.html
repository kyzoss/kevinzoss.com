How to Play

01

Pass the phone to the artist. Everyone else closes their eyes.

02

Pick a card. The picture shows what to draw, so little ones don't need to read. Tap the speaker to hear it quietly.

03

Put the phone down where everyone can see and draw with your finger. No letters or numbers.

04

Shout your guesses. When someone gets it, the artist taps Got it.

05

Tap the full screen button to turn the whole phone into paper. Tap Tools to bring the toolbar back.

05b

No time limit and Draw first are in game settings. With Draw first, the artist draws in secret with no clock, taps Ready, and the guess clock starts when everyone sees it.

06

Star Race: the guesser and the artist both get a star. Team Family: everyone works together to fill the rainbow.

07

Stuck? Hints unlock as the timer runs down. Forgot your word? Hold the eye button to peek.

08

Characters: build one from parts, stamp it on the page and draw a world around it. Turn on Character Adventures and you draw what your character is doing.

09

Grown-ups: Share, then Add to Home Screen makes it a full-screen app that works offline.
