How to play

Choose an age level, then a game. Each question shows a card with big answer buttons. Tap one: green means right, orange means try again next time (the right answer lights up too). Tap Next to flip the next card.

Quiz run

10 or 20 questions. The game remembers which cards you have seen so they do not come back until you have played them all. Score 60 percent for two stars and 90 percent for three.

Two players

Two to four players pick a name, an animal friend and their own level, then take turns answering. Each player's cards come from their own level. The player with the most right answers wins.

Practice

Pick one topic and keep going as long as you like. Stop whenever you want to see your score.

Tap the speaker to have the question read out loud, if your device can speak.
