The operating system

How I actually work

  • Agent teams, not chat windows. My AI runs as five-agent companies with lanes, worklogs, and a boss agent that reports to me.
  • Verification before "done." Every agent has to prove its work ran, with tests, checks and receipts, or say "unverified." No confident nonsense.
  • Loops over prompts. Recurring work becomes a scheduled loop: weekly reviews, campaign pacing, portfolio health. It runs whether I remember or not.
  • Ship small, ship real. Every venture gets a live product fast, then earns its next feature. Demos don't count.
